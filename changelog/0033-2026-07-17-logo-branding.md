# Changelog 0033 — Custom brand logo + web branding

- **Timestamp:** 2026-07-17 (AST, UTC-4)
- **Requested by:** Arif
- **Task:** Give the app a proper logo and consistent branding across the app and
  the browser tab. (A "cyber" hexagon variant was tried and rejected in-session;
  reverted to the mark below before any commit.)
- **Status:** Applied; `flutter analyze` clean; verified in-browser (login,
  dashboard app bar, enlarged favicon, generated PNG).

## What changed
- **`widgets/app_logo.dart` (new):** a reusable `AppLogo` drawn with a
  `CustomPainter` — a rounded amber badge holding a "team hierarchy" glyph (one
  lead node linked to two report nodes), reading as people + structure (brands →
  stores → staff). Crisp at any size, no image assets. Replaces the stock
  `Icons.groups_2`-in-a-square used before.
- **Shared everywhere:** login wordmark, server-setup wordmark, and the
  **dashboard app bar** (which previously had no mark). Removed the duplicated
  inline logo widgets in login/server-setup.
- **Web branding:** `web/favicon.svg` (new, matching the mark) + regenerated
  `favicon.png` and the PWA icons (`Icon-192/512`, `Icon-maskable-192/512`) to
  match; `web/index.html` title → "Staff Portal" (was "staff_frontend"), real
  description, `theme-color`, apple title, SVG-favicon link (PNG fallback kept);
  `web/manifest.json` name/short_name/colors/description updated.
